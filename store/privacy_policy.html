Privacy Policy — Blur Anything

Last updated: April 2026

Data collection

Blur Anything collects no data of any kind. The extension does not transmit, store, or share any information about you, your browsing activity, or the content of pages you visit.

Permissions

The extension requests the activeTab permission solely to draw blur overlays on the current page when you activate it. This permission is never used to read page content, capture screenshots, or send data anywhere.

Third parties

No third-party services, analytics tools, or advertising SDKs are included.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue on the GitHub repository or contact the developer directly.
