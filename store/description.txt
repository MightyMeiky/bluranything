SHORT DESCRIPTION (≤132 chars)
──────────────────────────────
Click and drag to blur any area on any page. Click a blurred area to remove it. No data collected.

LONG DESCRIPTION
──────────────────────────────
Blur Anything lets you instantly blur sensitive information on any webpage — usernames, balances, messages, addresses — before sharing your screen or taking a screenshot.

HOW IT WORKS
• Click the extension icon to enter blur mode (indigo border appears)
• Click and drag over any area to blur it
• Click a blurred area to remove it
• Press Escape to exit blur mode

USE CASES
• Hide personal info during screen shares or recordings
• Blur prices, balances, or account numbers before screenshots
• Protect private messages or emails visible on screen
• Anonymize content before sharing with colleagues

PRIVACY
Blur Anything collects zero data. No analytics, no tracking, no network requests. Everything happens locally in your browser. The extension only activates when you click the icon.

PERMISSIONS
• activeTab — needed to draw blur overlays on the current page. No other permissions requested.
